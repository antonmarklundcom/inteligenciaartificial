# ADR 0001 — News section and particle-brain home page

**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Status:** Accepted by the owner's request; pending the review in `14_IMPLEMENTATION_PHASES.md`

## Context

`00_EXECUTIVE_DECISIONS.md` §0.1 lists "an AI news blog" under *what we explicitly are
not*, and D11 specifies MDX in-repo. The owner asked for a news stream ("a lot of AI
content news") that a content pipeline can feed, and for the particle-brain home design.

## Decision

1. Add `/noticias` next to `/blog` (guides). News is not the business: every item must
   say what changes for a Paraguayan company and link to a service or tool. The site
   stays services-led.
2. Content lives as Markdown with validated front-matter in `content/` (see
   `content/README.md`) instead of MDX: a pipeline can write plain files, and the build
   rejects news without sources.
3. The home page uses a WebGL particle scene (three 0.149.0, pinned). Its data
   (`public/brain/shapes.bin`) is generated by `scripts/build-brain-data.mjs`.
4. The calculator (D8) ships as a client-side tool; nothing is stored, which is stricter
   than the bucketed storage allowed in `04_TOOL_PORTFOLIO.md`.

## Consequences

- R5/§0.1 risk: news without commercial intent dilutes the site. Mitigation: the
  editorial rules in `content/README.md`, and sources are mandatory.
- `06_SEO_ARCHITECTURE.md` §10.6 requires a named human author. Articles are currently
  signed by the organisation; a named author must be added before launch.
- Not built yet: `/nosotros`, the Diagnóstico, the prompt library, a lead form backed by
  the database.
